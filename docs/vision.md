# Black Sea Eco Monitor

## Project Vision

Black Sea Eco Monitor is an AI-powered environmental intelligence platform designed to monitor, analyze and visualize ecological events in the Black Sea region.

The system combines satellite observations, maritime tracking, open-source intelligence, environmental data and AI agents into a unified geospatial monitoring environment.

The main goal is to create a digital environmental awareness system capable of detecting potential ecological threats, analyzing their causes and presenting actionable information through an interactive 3D map interface.

---

# Mission

Create a digital twin of the Black Sea ecosystem that helps researchers, environmental organizations and decision makers understand ecological changes in near real time.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Data Fusion

The platform combines multiple heterogeneous data sources:

- satellite imagery;
- maritime traffic data;
- environmental sensors;
- weather information;
- open-source intelligence;
- public reports.

No single data source is treated as absolute truth.

The system combines multiple signals to increase confidence.

---

## 2. AI-Assisted Environmental Intelligence

AI agents collect, process and analyze information from open sources.

Agents help:

- discover environmental incidents;
- summarize information;
- detect anomalies;
- correlate events;
- estimate confidence levels.

---

## 3. Geospatial Visualization

All information is represented as geographic events:

Examples:

- oil spills;
- wildfires;
- pollution events;
- unusual maritime activity;
- extreme weather events.

Events are displayed on an interactive 3D map.

---

# Initial Target Region

Black Sea coastline:

- Russia;
- Georgia;
- Turkey;
- Bulgaria;
- Romania;
- Ukraine.

The first prototype focuses on the Russian Black Sea coast.

---

# Initial Use Cases

## 1. Oil Pollution Monitoring

Detection and analysis of:

- oil spills;
- possible pollution sources;
- vessel activity near affected areas;
- public reports.

---

## 2. Wildfire Monitoring

Monitoring:

- active fires;
- fire propagation risks;
- weather conditions;
- nearby infrastructure.

---

## 3. Maritime Environmental Awareness

Analysis of:

- vessel traffic;
- suspicious activity;
- ecological risks connected with maritime operations.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Black Sea Eco Monitor can become a foundation for:

- environmental digital twins;
- emergency response systems;
- educational platforms;
- VR/AR ecological simulations;
- AI-powered environmental analytics.
